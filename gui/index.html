Query Help

Type a word or a few words, then Search. Results match the post text and any notes or keywords saved with it.

  • Several words — a post must include all of them. sleep apnea only finds posts that mention both sleep and apnea.
  • Either word — put capital OR between them. fertility OR TFR finds posts with either word.
  • Exact phrase — wrap it in quotes. "interest rates" keeps those words together, in that order.
  • Starts with — add a star at the end. fertil* matches fertility, fertilizer, and similar starts.
  • Leave a word out — capital NOT after what you want. diet NOT keto drops posts that also say keto.
  • Mix them — use parentheses. (sleep OR insomnia) NOT mattress
